### Week 1, lecture notes

What is Psychology?
Psychology is the scientific study of behavior and mental processes, divided into several distinct 'schools of thought':

  • Structuralism: Focus on introspection and consciousness elements (Wilhelm Wundt).
  • Functionalism: How organisms adapt, survive, and flourish (William James).
  • Behaviorism: Objective observables rather than inner state (John B. Watson, B.F. Skinner).
